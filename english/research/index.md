#### World model-based object search
* We study techniques that use world models to predict the state of unobserved spaces and plan, in real time, exploration paths that efficiently find target objects specified in natural language.

#### Embodied AI agents
* We study learning methods that enable embodied agents in virtual or real environments to perceive their surroundings, set their own goals, act autonomously, and adapt to new situations through experience.

#### Wide-area camera tracking and 3D reconstruction
* Using video captured with mobile devices, we study techniques that track the camera's position and orientation in real time and reconstruct the 3D structure of the surroundings at large-scale industrial sites.

#### 3DGS+SLAM
* We study techniques that speed up SLAM mapping by efficiently placing initial Gaussians using image edges and local structure, reducing optimization cost while maintaining rendering quality.

#### Ray/Path Tracing
* We study methods that generate photorealistic 3D images by simulating physical light interactions such as reflection and transmission, and combine them with denoising to improve the quality and efficiency of real-time rendering.

#### Physics-based simulation
* We study simulation techniques that accurately and efficiently reproduce a wide range of physical phenomena, including object deformation and motion, fluid flow, and collisions between objects.
